# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes Superstore retail sales data using Python and Tableau.

The project aims to identify sales trends, profitability drivers, customer behavior, product performance, and regional performance to support data-driven business decisions.

# Key Findings

### 📈 Sales Growth

- Sales increased from $484K in 2014 to $733K in 2017.
- Profit increased from $49K to $93K.
- The business demonstrated consistent growth throughout the period.

### 🌎 Regional Performance

- West generated the highest profit ($108K).
- East was the second most profitable region.
- Central generated the lowest profit.

### 📦 Product Performance

- Technology generated the highest sales ($836K).
- Furniture and Office Supplies followed closely behind.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

- Consumer customers contributed the highest revenue.
- Corporate and Home Office segments generated lower sales.

---

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Invest More in Technology Products

Technology products generated the highest sales and should remain a strategic focus.

### 2. Replicate Best Practices from the West Region

Successful strategies from the West region can be applied to lower-performing regions.

### 3. Improve Low-Profit Regions

Further analysis should be conducted to identify factors affecting profitability in Central and South regions.

### 4. Strengthen Customer Retention

Focus on retaining Consumer customers through loyalty programs and personalized marketing.

### 5. Optimize Discount Strategy

Monitor discount policies carefully to maintain healthy profit margins.
